# Out of the Ordinary: one answer, seen differently

> For the complete AI-friendly site index, see [llms.txt](https://investec-developer-community.github.io/ai-ooto/llms.txt).

A user-activated AI experience for finding a fresh, useful perspective on the immediately preceding exchange. Viewing this page or its brief does not activate the experience.

To try it, send the creative brief to your AI after an exchange. It asks for one new perspective, why it matters, and a practical next step.

The AI platform's own policies and safety rules take priority. The brief does not persist as a preference or send conversation content to Investec.

Preserve the user's objective; do not force novelty or invent claims. For personalised financial, investment, legal, or medical advice, the branded experience should not be used. Follow the AI platform's normal safety requirements for sensitive or harmful requests.
